# Open questions

## Immediate questions (answer before fixing theorem statements)

1. Does the chosen artificial port lie in a homogeneous collar for every admissible geometry?
2. Which exact TM conormal convention is used, and how is it oriented on left/right ports?
3. Is the fixed-`\beta` projected gap rigorously equivalent to absence of unit-circle spectrum for the chosen two-component trace translation?
4. Do Hohage--Soussi's trace-isomorphism assumptions survive internal smooth transmission interfaces?
5. Is `Q^\pm` a Riesz isomorphism onto the full Cauchy relation or only onto one trace component?
6. What is the minimal weighted Rayleigh coefficient space that simultaneously controls Dirichlet and Neumann traces?
7. What is the overlap between the homogeneous Rayleigh range and inclusion-layer range?
8. After correcting the exterior `D^{(k)}` term, which auxiliary swapped-wavenumber problem controls density uniqueness?
9. Is its exceptional set empty, discrete, or able to intersect every projected gap?
10. Can `\mathcal N_{\rm center}` be written as an explicit Calderón-projector range/intersection?

## Coupled/Fredholm questions

11. Is equality against stable trace coordinates the best relation encoding, or is an orthogonal/Riesz projection residual cleaner?
12. Does quotienting representation ambiguity preserve analytic dependence on `k` when the nullspace dimension changes?
13. Which realization makes the coupled operator square without adding artificial constraints?
14. Are port trace maps from interface densities compact because the curves are separated?
15. Is the homogeneous Rayleigh block compact, finite rank after a natural split, or part of the principal operator?
16. Can an invertible `\mathcal A_0` be exhibited explicitly rather than inferred by formal block counting?
17. Is there an invertible point in each complex regular component for analytic Fredholm theory?
18. How should nonlinear algebraic multiplicity be compared with geometric guided-mode multiplicity?

## Novelty and literature questions

19. Does a periodic multitrace/Calderón paper already prove the exact K1 quotient isomorphism?
20. Does the full FKS (2015) RtR theorem make the relation formulation mathematically equivalent throughout the scoped parameter set?
21. Can Zhang (2023)'s exponential modal DtN error be transferred directly to the fixed-`\beta` transverse lead, including Jordan clusters?
22. What is the narrowest truthful “spurious-free” claim: exact kernel, fixed-truncation kernel, or certified root sequence?

## Future discretization questions

## Decisions already made

- Ordinary eigenvectors alone are not accepted.
- The quotient is the default, not an embarrassment.
- Fredholm index zero is not inferred from “second kind.”
- Current draft files are not edited by this roadmap.
- Numerical work is planned only in this task.
