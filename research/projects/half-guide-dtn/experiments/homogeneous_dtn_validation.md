## Formula Check

For a homogeneous channel, the cell outward-normal blocks are `T00=T11=gamma*cot(gamma*L)` and `T01=T10=-gamma*csc(gamma*L)`. The project port DtN is `-(T00+T01*R)` and should equal `1i*gamma`.

## Interpretation

The converted project DtN should match `diag(1i*gamma_m)`. The Joly-style coefficient has the opposite sign in this homogeneous right-port convention because it appears naturally in a boundary condition `partial_x u + K u = 0`.

In the lossless case, propagating channels have unit-modulus multipliers and therefore are not selected by the absorbing criterion `rho(R)<1`. Adding absorption moves those multipliers inside the unit disk and makes stable selection unambiguous away from thresholds.
