# Paper 007

**Title:** Riesz Bases and Jordan Form of the Translation Operator in Semi-Infinite Periodic Waveguides  
**Authors:** Thorsten Hohage, Sofiane Soussi  
**Year / journal:** 2013, *Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées* 100(1), 113--135  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.matpur.2012.10.013  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matpur.2012.10.013  
**Local file:** `ref/Hohage2013.pdf`  
**Search route:** Local seed; modal completeness queries; forward chain.

1. **Geometry:** Semi-infinite periodic strip.
2. **PDE/polarization:** Acoustic or TM scalar time-harmonic wave equation; Dirichlet, Neumann, and mixed wall cases.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Frequency fixed; translation eigenvalues/Floquet exponents are modal parameters.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** A solution space (V) and trace space (H_\tau); radiation encoded by selected Floquet modes.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Trace bases can represent admissible half-guide data and hence a modal boundary operator.
6. **Discretization:** The paper is primarily analytic, not a complete numerical solver.
7. **Theory:** Theorem 2.2 proves a Riesz basis of Floquet modes, representation of translation by an infinite Jordan matrix, and trace Riesz bases; only finitely many Jordan blocks have size (>1).
8. **Numerics:** No central line-defect BIE study.
9. **Assumptions:** Periodic coefficient/guide and trace map isomorphism hypotheses detailed near Theorem 2.2 and Proposition 6.2.
10. **Limitations:** Does not give draft's scattering-matrix discretization or its error.
11. **Draft relation:** Conjecture 1 is overstrong/incomplete because it spans only ordinary decaying Bloch eigenvectors. The correct formulation must include generalized Floquet modes/Jordan chains and specify the trace topology.
12. **Exact passages:** Theorem 2.2, discussion immediately after it (“proves the conjectures in [17, Remark 5.1]”), Sections 3.5 and 6, Appendix A.
13. **Assessment:** **Already solved in a stronger generalized form** under stated assumptions; current draft wording is likely false at defective multipliers.
