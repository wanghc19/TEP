# Paper 011

**Title:** Helmholtz Equation in Periodic Media with a Line Defect  
**Authors:** Jérémy Coatléven  
**Year / journal:** 2012, *Journal of Computational Physics* 231(4), 1675--1704  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.jcp.2011.10.022  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2011.10.022  
**Local file:** `ref/Coatleven2012.pdf`  
**Search route:** Local seed; backward chain.

1. **Geometry:** Periodic line defect with possibly different left and right periodic media.
2. **PDE/polarization:** Absorbed scalar Helmholtz.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Frequency fixed for forced scattering; transverse FBT quasi-momentum integrated.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** Absorption supplies uniqueness and exponential decay.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Lippmann--Schwinger plus exact cell DtN; compact propagation/Riccati operator.
6. **Discretization:** Floquet--Bloch quadrature and bounded-domain/FEM solves.
7. **Theory:** Theorems 4.3--4.6 construct propagation/DtN; Theorems 5.1--5.6 establish well-posed approximation and convergence estimates.
8. **Numerics:** Convergence and complexity studies for forced fields.
9. **Assumptions:** Positive absorption and periodic exterior media.
10. **Limitations:** Remark 5.5 states the no-absorption case needs a more complete framework.
11. **Draft relation:** Prior art for different leads, absorbed DtN, Riccati propagation, and an actual error analysis. It does not solve the lossless guided-mode eigenproblem or BIE coupling.
12. **Exact passages:** Theorems 4.3--4.6, 5.1--5.6; Remarks 5.3--5.5; Section 9.
13. **Assessment:** **Already solved** for absorbed scattering/reduction; **technically different** from lossless eigenmodes.
