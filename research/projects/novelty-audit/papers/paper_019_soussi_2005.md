# Paper 019

**Title:** Convergence of the Supercell Method for Defect Modes Calculations in Photonic Crystals  
**Authors:** Sofiane Soussi  
**Year / journal:** 2005, *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis* 43(3), 1175--1201  
**DOI:** 10.1137/040616875  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1137/040616875  
**Local file:** None  
**Search route:** Backward chain from Fliss and difficult-regime search.

1. **Geometry:** Compact defects in 2-D photonic crystals; supercell truncation.
2. **PDE/polarization:** TE and TM scalar reductions.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Defect frequency in a background gap.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** Localization is approximated by a periodically repeated supercell.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Large periodic supercell rather than exact transparent boundary.
6. **Discretization:** Standard cell eigenproblem; abstract convergence analysis.
7. **Theory:** Proves exponential convergence of defect frequencies with supercell size and characterizes background spectral pollution.
8. **Numerics:** Supports supercell use for well-localized defect modes.
9. **Assumptions:** Compact defect and gap localization; line defects require axial Floquet reduction and different transverse interpretation.
10. **Limitations:** Poorly confined/band-edge modes require very large cells; the geometry is principally local defect.
11. **Draft relation:** Benchmark method and error-analysis standard; the draft needs controlled evidence that its transparent method improves near band edges.
12. **Exact passages:** SIAM abstract and principal convergence theorems, pp. 1175--1201.
13. **Assessment:** **Established comparator**, not direct overlap with the draft BIE.
