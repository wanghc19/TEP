# Paper 008

**Title:** Spectrum Decomposition of Translation Operators in Periodic Waveguide  
**Authors:** Ruming Zhang  
**Year / journal:** 2021, *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* 81(1), 233--257  
**DOI:** 10.1137/19M1290942  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1290942  
**Local file:** None  
**Search route:** Generalized-eigenfunction query; forward chain from Hohage--Soussi.

1. **Geometry:** Periodic waveguide and translation across cells.
2. **PDE/polarization:** Scalar periodic Helmholtz scattering setting.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Translation/Floquet multipliers at fixed physical frequency.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** Limiting-absorption solution.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Translation operator is decomposed by contour/residue analysis.
6. **Discretization:** Analytic spectral decomposition rather than a direct line-defect eigensolver.
7. **Theory:** The operator and LAP solution are expressed through eigenvalues and generalized eigenfunctions related to Bloch waves.
8. **Numerics:** Illustrative periodic-waveguide field decompositions.
9. **Assumptions:** LAP and isolated characteristic values in the relevant contour; exact hypotheses are in the SIAM paper.
10. **Limitations:** Scattering problem, not a central guided-mode nonlinear eigenproblem.
11. **Draft relation:** Reinforces that stable half-guide traces need generalized eigenfunctions; provides a rigorous route beyond Conjecture 1.
12. **Exact passages:** Abstract; main spectrum-decomposition theorems in Sections 3--5; SIAM J. Appl. Math. 81, pp. 233--257.
13. **Assessment:** **Already solved** at the continuous generalized-modal decomposition level; draft finite scattering pencil still needs a convergence bridge.
