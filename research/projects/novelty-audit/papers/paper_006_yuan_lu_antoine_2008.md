# Paper 006

**Title:** Modeling Photonic Crystals by Boundary Integral Equations and Dirichlet-to-Neumann Maps  
**Authors:** Jianhua Yuan, Ya Yan Lu, Xavier Antoine  
**Year / journal:** 2008, *Journal of Computational Physics* 227(9), 4617--4629  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.jcp.2008.01.014  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2008.01.014  
**Local file:** None  
**Search route:** Required unit-cell BIE/DtN query; Elsevier full record.

1. **Geometry:** 2-D periodic bulk cells with dielectric cylinders of arbitrary cross section.
2. **PDE/polarization:** E and H scalar polarizations; piecewise-constant dielectric coefficients.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Frequency or Bloch-wave-vector component for band structure.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** Unit-cell Bloch conditions, no open half-guide.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Boundary integral scattering solutions are used to construct a matrix approximation to the unit-cell DtN map.
6. **Discretization:** Interface BIE and boundary sampling/special solutions.
7. **Theory:** Establishes practical BIE construction of cell DtN maps and smaller boundary eigenproblems; the introduction compares transfer, scattering, and DtN formulations and notes transfer instability.
8. **Numerics:** Elliptic/kite-shaped rods and holes; band structures for both polarizations.
9. **Assumptions:** Arbitrary smooth cross section, piecewise homogeneous cells; finite boundary basis must span cell solutions accurately.
10. **Limitations:** Bulk bands and finite devices, not the line-defect guided-mode eigenproblem.
11. **Draft relation:** Direct prior art for “BIE instead of FEM,” cell DtN, TE/TM weighting, and boundary-only discretization. A draft contribution must be the exact coupling/certification or a new regime, not that substitution alone.
12. **Exact passages:** Abstract; Introduction; Sections 2--4; JCP 227, pp. 4617--4629.
13. **Assessment:** **Already solved** for BIE unit-cell DtN; **technically different** geometry from periodic half-guides.
