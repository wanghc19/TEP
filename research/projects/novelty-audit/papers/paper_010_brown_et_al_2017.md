# Paper 010

**Title:** Gap Localization of TE-Modes by Arbitrarily Weak Defects  
**Authors:** B. M. Brown, V. Hoang, M. Plum, M. Radosz, I. Wood  
**Year / journal:** 2017, *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* 95, 942--962  
**DOI:** 10.1112/jlms.12046  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12046  
**Local file:** None  
**Search route:** Required TE/divergence-form query; Wiley full record.

1. **Geometry:** A line defect in a 2-D periodic background, periodic along the defect and localized transversely.
2. **PDE/polarization:** TE divergence-form operator with discontinuous dielectric coefficients.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Eigenvalues introduced into a background band gap after axial Floquet reduction.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** Quasiperiodic axial functions and transverse localization.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Birman--Schwinger/Green-operator variational analysis, not a transparent boundary.
6. **Discretization:** None central; theoretical existence paper.
7. **Theory:** Theorem 2.1 gives a sufficient weak-defect localization condition; coefficients may be discontinuous.
8. **Numerics:** Not an algorithmic guided-mode computation paper.
9. **Assumptions:** Gap and band-edge hypotheses plus weak defect conditions.
10. **Limitations/open questions:** Conclusion mentions degenerate band edges and full Maxwell as harder extensions.
11. **Draft relation:** Shows that TE line defects and discontinuous principal coefficients are not themselves novel. It does not provide the draft's BIE/transparent numerical formulation.
12. **Exact passages:** Theorem 2.1, Remark 2.2, Sections 4--5, Section 7 conclusion, pp. 942--962.
13. **Assessment:** **Already solved** for TE weak-defect existence; an interface-only unified TM/TE algorithm could still be partially new.
