2. **PDE/polarization:** Scalar Helmholtz scattering.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Fixed physical frequency; generalized modal multipliers define DtN.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** LAP/generalized-eigenfunction outgoing expansion.

6. **Discretization:** Truncated modal DtN plus FEM.
7. **Theory:** Finite modal truncation converges exponentially; the full numerical method has an error estimate.
8. **Numerics:** Demonstrates effective truncation of complicated unbounded guides.
9. **Assumptions:** Spectral separation/exponential decay conditions and regular FEM hypotheses.
10. **Limitations:** Forced scattering and FEM, not a Müller line-defect eigenproblem; exceptional unit-circle/threshold regimes remain delicate.
