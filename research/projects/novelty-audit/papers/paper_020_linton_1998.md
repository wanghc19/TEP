**Title:** The Green's Function for the Two-Dimensional Helmholtz Equation in Periodic Domains  
**Authors:** C. M. Linton  
**Year / journal:** 1998, *Journal of Engineering Mathematics* 33(4), 377--402  
**DOI:** 10.1023/A:1004377501747  
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004377501747  
**Local file:** `ref/Linton1998.pdf`  
**Search route:** Local seed; QP Green-function chain.

1. **Geometry:** One-dimensionally periodic 2-D Helmholtz domains and parallel-plate waveguides.
2. **PDE/polarization:** Scalar Helmholtz.
3. **Spectral parameter:** Wavenumber and quasiperiodic phase.
4. **Spaces/radiation:** Outgoing periodic Green function/Rayleigh interpretation.
5. **Exterior reduction:** Analytic Green kernel for periodic BIEs.
6. **Discretization:** Kummer transformations, integral/lattice-sum and Ewald evaluation.
7. **Theory:** Derives equivalent rapidly convergent representations and documents exceptional/slowly convergent behavior.
8. **Numerics:** Accuracy/speed comparisons for the alternative formulas.
9. **Assumptions:** Nonexceptional parameters for standard representations.
10. **Limitations:** Kernel evaluation only; no line-defect eigenproblem or modern proxy method.
11. **Draft relation:** Supplies the reference values used by the draft MFS benchmark; agreement with Linton validates implementation but is not a guided-mode contribution.
12. **Exact passages:** Sections 2--5, pp. 377--402.
13. **Assessment:** **Already solved** analytic Green-function background; draft MFS is an implementation alternative.
