## Cross-paper conclusions from the local corpus

- The line-defect guided-mode problem and its exact bounded nonlinear DtN
  reduction are already explicit in Fliss (2013), with numerical realization in
  the cited Klindworth--Schmidt--Fliss work outside the local folder.
- Joly--Li--Fliss (2006) and Coatléven (2012) already establish the absorbed
  propagation-operator/Riccati machinery; neither claims a complete lossless
  theory.
- Hohage--Soussi (2013) closes the completeness question in a generalized
  Floquet/Jordan basis. This is stronger and more precise than draft
  Conjecture 1 as presently worded.
- Barnett--Greengard (2010) is the closest source for the draft's QP/free-space
  representation and complementary swapped-wavenumber uniqueness proof.
- Luan--Sun--Zhuang (2019), Barnett--Greengard (2010), and Linton (1998)
  collectively leave little room to claim the MFS/QP Green-function construction
  itself as new without a new theorem, stability result, or difficult-regime
  capability.
