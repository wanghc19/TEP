1. **Geometry:** finite-width periodic rod slab, homogeneous exterior.
2. **PDE/polarization:** 2D Maxwell scalar reductions.
3. **Parameter:** real Bloch beta, complex frequency omega.
4. **Class:** real bound state versus complex resonance.
5. **Radiation:** outgoing Rayleigh continuation with `exp(-i omega t)`.
6. **Periodic leads:** periodic only along slab; exterior is homogeneous.
7. **Multiplier selection:** beta-quasiperiodicity; Rayleigh branches externally.
8. **Method:** Calderón boundary-integral projections, Fredholm second kind.
9. **NEP:** nontrivial nullspace defines `omega=W(beta)`.
10. **Eigensolver:** determinant/nullspace tracking in Section 7.
11. **Main theorem:** Theorems 5.1--5.2 (paper pp. 333--334) relate real
    frequencies to bound states and complex frequencies to resonant states;
    Section 6 links them to scattering.
12. **Convergence:** Fredholm analysis plus numerical examples, no full Nyström

13. **Branches:** analytic domain excludes Rayleigh branch singularities.
14. **Spurious roots:** second-kind projections and field reconstruction.
15. **Numerics:** Section 7, channel defect and surface-wave examples.
16. **Limitations:** no infinite periodic half-lead trace relation.
17. **Draft relation:** strongest direct precedent for a unified central BIE

18. **Reusable theory:** analytic Fredholm projection and time-sign convention.
19. **Reusable numerics:** root continuation and scattering verification.
