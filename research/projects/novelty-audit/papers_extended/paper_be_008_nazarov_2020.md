Title: Threshold Resonances and Virtual Levels in the Spectrum of Cylindrical and Periodic Waveguides  
Authors: Sergei A. Nazarov  
Year: 2020  
Journal / archive: Izvestiya: Mathematics 84(6), 1105--1160  
DOI: 10.1070/IM8928

Search route: required threshold-resonance query and forward citation chain.

1. **Geometry:** cylindrical and periodic outlets to infinity.
2. **PDE/polarization:** general self-adjoint second-order elliptic systems;
   quantum, acoustic, and elastic examples.
3. **Parameter:** real spectral parameter approaching a continuous-spectrum
   threshold from below or above.
4. **Class:** ordinary/degenerate threshold, virtual level, threshold resonance.
5. **Radiation:** separated asymptotics and flux forms in weighted spaces.
6. **Periodic lead:** periodic outlet modes and polynomial almost-standing waves.
7. **Multiplier selection:** standing zero-flux chains, beyond a modulus rule.
8. **Exterior method:** self-adjoint extensions in weighted spaces.
9. **NEP:** threshold as eigenvalue of an extended operator, not a matrix NEP.
10. **Eigensolver:** none.
11. **Main result:** Section 2 constructs polynomial waves; equations
    (2.26), (2.29), and (2.36) evaluate their flux pairings. Later sections
    classify threshold resonances and virtual levels (journal pp. 1121--1152).
12. **Convergence:** asymptotic/functional analysis, not discretization.
13. **Thresholds:** central subject; degenerate thresholds can have polynomially
    growing standing waves and effects absent in scalar cylindrical guides.
14. **Spurious roots:** not applicable.
15. **Numerics:** none central.
16. **Limitations:** not tailored to dielectric-cylinder BIEs.
17. **Draft relation:** proves that “threshold = slow exponential decay” is false
    in general and motivates a linear relation with generalized traces.
18. **Reusable theory:** weighted spaces, flux pairing, virtual-level taxonomy.
19. **Reusable numerics:** exact asymptotic basis targets.
20. **Assessment:** essential threshold theory; applying it is a major new task.
