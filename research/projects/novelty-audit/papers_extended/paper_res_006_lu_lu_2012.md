Search route: BIE leaky-mode forward chain.

1. **Geometry:** multi-inclusion photonic-crystal fiber cross-section.
2. **PDE/polarization:** full-vector Maxwell waveguide modes.
3. **Parameter:** fixed frequency, complex propagation constant beta.
4. **Class:** guided and leaky fiber modes.
5. **Radiation:** outgoing transverse Hankel solutions.
6. **Periodic leads:** none; longitudinal invariance.
7. **Multiplier selection:** not applicable.
8. **Method:** high-order two-unknown BIE.
9. **NEP:** equation (1), `F(beta) phi=0`.
10. **Eigensolver:** nonlinear root search.
11. **Main result:** BIE derivation in Section II, discretization in Section III,
    numerical tests in Section IV (pp. 1611--1615).
12. **Convergence:** exponential numerical convergence reported.
13. **Branches:** transverse wavenumbers involve square roots of `k_j^2-beta^2`.
14. **Spurious roots:** cross-order convergence and field validation.
15. **Numerics:** PCFs with many air holes and small leakage loss.
16. **Limitations:** not the periodic lead/complex-frequency problem.
17. **Draft relation:** proves high-order BIE leaky-mode computation is prior art.
18. **Reusable theory:** interface-only NEP structure.
19. **Reusable numerics:** convergence protocol for tiny imaginary parts.
20. **Assessment:** substantial component overlap.
