1. **Geometry:** two-sided closed periodic waveguide.
2. **PDE/polarization:** scalar time-harmonic wave equation.
3. **Parameter:** real frequency with an absorptive limiting problem.
4. **Class:** propagating/evanescent outgoing modes; stationary set separated.
5. **Radiation:** Definition 5, p. 32, equation (60).
6. **Periodic leads:** Bloch transform and band functions over the periodic cell.
7. **Multiplier selection:** propagating modes are selected by the sign of band
   derivative/group velocity; stationary points form `Xi_0`.
8. **Exterior method:** modal/asymptotic radiation condition.
9. **NEP:** none.
10. **Eigensolver:** none.
11. **Main theorem:** Theorem 7 gives the far-field expansion with exponentially
    decaying remainder; Theorem 8, pp. 32--34, proves well-posedness when
    `omega^2` is outside the excluded point spectrum.
12. **Convergence:** limiting-absorption/asymptotic convergence, no mesh estimate.
13. **Thresholds:** zero derivative modes are exceptional, not silently assigned
    outgoing direction.
14. **Spurious roots:** not applicable.
15. **Numerics:** illustrative band structure, not a BIE mode solver.
16. **Limitations:** the exact spectral-edge case is outside the clean Theorem 8
    regime; Remark 11 weakens the remainder condition but not the assumptions.
17. **Draft relation:** invalidates discarding all unit-circle multipliers and
    motivates flux/group-velocity selection.
18. **Reusable theory:** Definition 5 and Theorems 7--8.
19. **Reusable numerics:** compute group velocity and test outgoing flux.
20. **Assessment:** substantial overlap for real-frequency radiation; not the
