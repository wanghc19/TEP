Title: Boundary-Integral Calculations of Two-Dimensional Electromagnetic Scattering in Infinite Photonic Crystal Slabs: Channel Defects and Resonances  
Authors: Mansoor A. Haider; Stephen P. Shipman; Stephanos Venakides  
Year: 2002  
Journal / archive: SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 62(6), 2129--2148

1. **Geometry:** finite propagation thickness, periodically repeated transverse
   slab with a channel defect.
2. **PDE/polarization:** E-polarized 2D Maxwell reduction.
3. **Parameter:** real-frequency driven scattering.
4. **Class:** resonant scattering peaks, not complex eigenvalues.
5. **Radiation:** periodic Green/Rayleigh far-field condition.
6. **Periodic leads:** transverse periodicity, homogeneous incident exterior.
7. **Multiplier selection:** incident/transmitted diffraction channels.
8. **Method:** first- and new second-kind BIE; Ewald/separable far field.
9. **NEP:** none explicitly.
10. **Eigensolver:** frequency scans.
11. **Main result:** second-kind formulation in Section 3; numerical resonance
    categories in Section 4, especially Figure 4.5 (journal pp. 2140--2146).
12. **Convergence:** comparison of first/second-kind results.
13. **Branches:** standard real-frequency QP Green domain.
14. **Spurious roots:** agreement of formulations, not a general root theorem.
15. **Numerics:** channel modes, band-edge high fields, transverse standing fields.
16. **Limitations:** scattering peaks are not certified resolvent poles.
17. **Draft relation:** almost the same physical vocabulary; prevents claiming
    novelty from BIE + channel defect + resonance alone.
18. **Reusable theory:** second-kind channel-defect representation.
19. **Reusable numerics:** transmission benchmark and resonance taxonomy.
20. **Assessment:** substantial overlap, but not the proposed complex periodic-
    lead NEP.
