1. **Geometry:** optical fibers/waveguides with many irregular inclusions.
2. **PDE/polarization:** full-vector Maxwell mode system.
3. **Parameter:** fixed frequency, complex propagation constant.
4. **Class:** bound, leaky, and complex modes.
5. **Radiation:** outgoing transverse Green functions.
6. **Periodic leads:** none.
7. **Multiplier selection:** not applicable.
8. **Method:** well-conditioned second-kind integral equation (SKIE).
9. **NEP:** boundary integral matrix depends nonlinearly on propagation constant.
10. **Eigensolver:** nonlinear singularity/root search.
11. **Main result:** SKIE is developed in Sections 2--3; discretization and
    examples occupy Sections 4--5 (journal pp. 650--662).
12. **Convergence:** extensive high-order numerical convergence and comparisons.
13. **Branches:** complex transverse wavenumbers must use outgoing sheets.
14. **Spurious roots:** second-kind conditioning plus cross-validation; no
    universal resonance no-pollution theorem.
15. **Numerics:** PCF, dielectric fibers, and silicon waveguides.
16. **Limitations:** translational, not transverse-periodic half-lead geometry.
17. **Draft relation:** strong BIE mode-solver prior art.
18. **Reusable theory:** second-kind vector interface formulation.
19. **Reusable numerics:** high-order loss/convergence tests.
20. **Assessment:** substantial component overlap.
