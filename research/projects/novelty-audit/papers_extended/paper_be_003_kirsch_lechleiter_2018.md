Title: A Radiation Condition Arising from the Limiting Absorption Principle for a Closed Full- or Half-Waveguide Problem

Year: 2018  
Journal / archive: Mathematical Methods in the Applied Sciences 41, 3955--3975

1. **Geometry:** closed full and half periodic waveguides.
2. **PDE/polarization:** scalar Helmholtz equation.
3. **Parameter:** real frequency; absorption used for physical selection.
4. **Class:** propagating plus evanescent outgoing solution.
5. **Radiation:** LAP-derived modal condition.
6. **Periodic leads:** Floquet--Bloch transform near exceptional values.
7. **Multiplier selection:** analytic Bloch-variable treatment, not a raw modulus
   threshold.
8. **Exterior method:** analytic Bloch expansion; no BIE.
9. **NEP:** none.
10. **Eigensolver:** none.
11. **Main result:** existence/uniqueness and equivalence of the LAP radiation
    condition; see Sections 3--5, especially the radiation definition and main
    well-posedness theorem in Section 5 (journal pp. 3966--3972).
12. **Convergence:** absorbed solutions converge locally; no discretization rate.
13. **Thresholds:** exceptional Floquet values require analytic local handling;
    an exact degenerate threshold is not a routine stable-subspace point.
14. **Spurious roots:** not applicable.
15. **Numerics:** none central.
16. **Limitations:** no dielectric-cylinder BIE or resonance sheet.
17. **Draft relation:** alternative foundation for real-axis outgoing traces.
18. **Reusable theory:** analytic treatment near exceptional Bloch variables.
19. **Reusable numerics:** continuation rather than sorting mode labels.
20. **Assessment:** partial overlap, important nondegeneracy warning.
