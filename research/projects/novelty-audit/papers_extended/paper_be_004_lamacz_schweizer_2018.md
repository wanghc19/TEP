   crystal; not a line-defect eigenproblem.
2. **PDE/polarization:** scalar Helmholtz with periodic coefficient.
3. **Parameter:** fixed real frequency.
4. **Class:** transmitted/outgoing Bloch waves.
5. **Radiation:** outgoing Bloch measure with positive energy flux.
6. **Periodic lead:** unbounded periodic crystal treated by Bloch expansion.
7. **Multiplier selection:** direction is flux/group velocity, not `|lambda|`.

9. **NEP:** none.
10. **Eigensolver:** cell Bloch spectral computations only.
11. **Main theorem:** weak uniqueness is proved in Section 4; the outgoing wave
    condition is introduced in Section 3 (journal pp. 1922--1934). The
    conservation/refraction result is in Section 5.
12. **Convergence:** limiting-domain sequence and Bloch-measure compactness.
13. **Thresholds:** assumptions avoid unresolved degeneracies; no universal
    zero-group-velocity selection is claimed.
14. **Spurious roots:** not applicable.
15. **Numerics:** schematic/dispersion examples.
16. **Limitations:** different geometry and a weak uniqueness statement.
17. **Draft relation:** supplies a flux criterion for unit-circle trace modes.
18. **Reusable theory:** Bloch measure and Poynting/flux selection.
19. **Reusable numerics:** biorthogonal flux normalization.
20. **Assessment:** adjacent but conceptually important.
