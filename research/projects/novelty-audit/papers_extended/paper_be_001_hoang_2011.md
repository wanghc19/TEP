1. **Geometry:** straight semi-infinite waveguide with periodic material.
2. **PDE/polarization:** scalar elliptic Helmholtz-type problem; no TE/TM claim.
3. **Parameter:** real spectral value in a band, regularized by small absorption.
4. **Class:** propagating-band outgoing solution, not a gap guided eigenmode.
5. **Radiation:** defined as the zero-absorption local limit.
6. **Periodic lead:** the whole semi-infinite exterior is periodic.
7. **Multiplier selection:** derived from Bloch spectral data, not merely modulus.
8. **Exterior method:** limiting absorption; no BIE/PML/RtR.
9. **NEP:** none.
10. **Eigensolver:** none.
11. **Main result:** local `H^1` convergence of absorbed solutions; see the main
    convergence result in the latter half of the paper, pp. 801--809, and the
    SIAM abstract. The public article page does not expose the theorem number,
    so no unverified theorem number is invented here.
12. **Error/convergence:** qualitative LAP convergence, not a discretization rate.
13. **Branches/thresholds:** band-interior analysis; exact stationary thresholds
    are not established by the abstract-level result.
14. **Spurious roots:** not applicable.
15. **Numerics:** not the main contribution.
16. **Limitations:** does not construct the current line-defect BIE trace pencil.
17. **Draft relation:** supplies the physical definition that an outgoing
    periodic-half-guide relation must reproduce in a propagation band.
18. **Reusable theory:** absorption-to-outgoing selection.
19. **Reusable numerics:** loss-to-zero validation experiment.
20. **Assessment:** substantial adjacent result; exact threshold remains open for
    the current formulation.
