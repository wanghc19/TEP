Search route: forward chain from generalized translation-spectrum papers.

1. **Geometry:** locally perturbed periodic waveguide.
2. **PDE/polarization:** scalar Helmholtz.
3. **Parameter:** real wavenumber with complex multiplier/Bloch variable.
4. **Class:** outgoing scattering in bands.
5. **Radiation:** deformed inverse Floquet--Bloch contour.
6. **Periodic lead:** cell problem meromorphic in multiplier `z`.
7. **Multiplier selection:** contour encloses right-going poles and excludes
   left-going poles.
8. **Exterior method:** Floquet transform plus FEM.
9. **NEP:** poles of the cell solution in `z`.
10. **Eigensolver:** contour deformation and finite elements.
11. **Main theorem:** Theorem 1 gives analytic/meromorphic continuation of the
    transformed field; Theorem 2 and equation (18) give its contour inverse.
12. **Convergence:** later sections prove finite-element approximation results.
13. **Thresholds:** exceptional sets and pole coincidences are treated by
    assumptions/extension in Section 8, not by crossing a branch point blindly.
14. **Spurious roots:** contour separates pole sets physically.
15. **Numerics:** scattering examples and convergence.
16. **Limitations:** real-frequency scattering, not complex-frequency resonance.
17. **Draft relation:** closest analytic multiplier-plane alternative to sorted
    Bloch eigenvectors.
18. **Reusable theory:** meromorphic cell family and Cauchy integral.
19. **Reusable numerics:** contour-defined outgoing projection.
20. **Assessment:** substantial overlap with the desired stable-relation
    machinery; complex-`k` extension remains nontrivial.
