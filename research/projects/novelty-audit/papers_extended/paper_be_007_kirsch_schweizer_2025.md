3. **Parameter:** real frequency plus several absorption models.

7. **Multiplier selection:** quasiperiodic modes under Assumption 3.5.
8. **Exterior method:** analytic/spectral theory.
9. **NEP:** none.

    homogeneous space.
12. **Convergence:** LAP convergence, no discretization estimate.
13. **Thresholds:** excluded/controlled by nondegeneracy; damping dependence is
    a direct warning against an unspecified “physical” limit.
14. **Spurious roots:** not applicable.
15. **Numerics:** Section 5 examples distinguish LAPs.
16. **Limitations:** no BIE or complex resonance.
17. **Draft relation:** requires the material loss model/time convention to be

18. **Reusable theory:** bounded-solution classification.
19. **Reusable numerics:** test multiple absorptive continuations.
20. **Assessment:** strong scope-limiting evidence.
