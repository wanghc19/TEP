2. **PDE/polarization:** scalar elliptic Helmholtz.
3. **Parameter:** real frequency in gap or continuous-spectrum regimes.
4. **Class:** scattering solutions and trapped modes.
5. **Radiation:** physically motivated outgoing modal/LAP condition.
6. **Periodic leads:** exact exterior DtN per outlet.
7. **Multiplier selection:** propagating modes by direction plus evanescent modes.
8. **Exterior method:** DtN coupled to bounded FEM.
9. **NEP:** trapped-mode frequencies are exceptional homogeneous solutions.

12. **Convergence:** approximation/error analysis in the numerical sections.
13. **Thresholds:** nondegeneracy/regular-frequency assumptions remain; a

15. **Numerics:** periodic-outlet examples.
16. **Limitations:** not an interface-only Müller formulation and not complex

19. **Reusable numerics:** independent FEM-DtN benchmark.
